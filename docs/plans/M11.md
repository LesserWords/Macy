# M11 Plan — White-Label Brand Composition & Cross-Workspace Persona Adaptation

**Milestone:** `M11` (Post-M10) · **ADR:** [ADR-034](../adr/ADR-034-white-label-brand-composition-and-persona-adaptation.md) · **Related Features:** `F01`, `F03`, `F12`, `F13`, `F14`, `F21`, `F22`, `F23` · **Status:** Planned (Test-First TDD)

## Goal
Enable multi-brand and white-label teams (for example, a core horizontal software brand plus vertical forks sharing ~70% of capabilities) to:
1. Compose brand fixtures cleanly at load/seed time without duplicating shared business rules or leaking parent/sibling product names.
2. Switch or adapt an approved article between isolated blogs targeting different buyer personas—**with zero direct runtime or database dependence between workspaces** and full SEO anti-duplication + deterministic compliance checks.

All implementation slices follow Macy's strict **Red → Green → Refactor** workflow (`ADR-018`, `ADR-019`, `ADR-020`), OpenSpec change validation, and `scripts/check.sh` universal gates (`100%` diff coverage on `engine/` + `plugins/`, `check_oss_hygiene.py`, `mypy --strict`, `lint-imports`).
