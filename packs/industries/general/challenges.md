# Key Content & Operational Challenges

- **Evidence vs. Opinion:** Separating verifiable primary statistics and institutional guidelines from unverified marketing assertions.
- **Search Discoverability & GEO Readiness:** Structuring headings, metadata, FAQs, and JSON-LD entities so both human readers and AI answer engines parse key takeaways accurately.
- **Editorial Consistency:** Maintaining brand voice, funnel-appropriate calls to action (`TOFU`, `MOFU`, `BOFU`), and strict compliance guardrails at scale.
