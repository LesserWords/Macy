# Standard Editorial Terminology

- **TOFU / MOFU / BOFU:** Top-, Middle-, and Bottom-of-Funnel content intent aligning educational depth with conversion readiness.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Structuring authoritative, source-grounded content and schemas for citation by AI search systems.
- **Claim Provenance:** Explicit linkage of every factual assertion to an extracted primary source document.
