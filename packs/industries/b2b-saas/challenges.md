# Key B2B SaaS Market & Operational Challenges

- **Build vs. Buy & Integration Sprawl:** Teams struggle with fragmented webhooks, brittle point-to-point scripts, and vendor lock-in.
- **Security, Privacy & Multi-Tenant Governance:** Enterprise buyers require strict tenant data isolation, encrypted secrets, and auditable change logs.
- **Demonstrating Measurable ROI Without Hype:** Standing out in crowded SaaS categories demands empirical benchmarks and reproducible workflows.
