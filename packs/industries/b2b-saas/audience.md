# Target Audience: B2B SaaS Buyers, Engineering Leaders & RevOps Teams

## Primary Personas
1. **CTOs, VPs of Engineering & Staff Architects:** Evaluate security posture, multi-tenant isolation, API reliability, and total cost of ownership.
2. **Product & RevOps Leaders:** Focus on onboarding velocity, workflow automation, integration ecosystems, and measurable retention metrics.
3. **Implementation & Security Evaluators:** Inspect SOC 2 / LGPD / GDPR readiness, SSO/OIDC support, audit trails, and operational runbooks.
